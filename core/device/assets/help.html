Finding your way around Android

  • All apps: on the home screen, drag upward from the lower part of the screen.
  • Home: the round button at the bottom, or F2.
  • Back: the triangle at the bottom, or Esc.
  • Recent apps: the square at the bottom. Drag an app upward to close it.
  • Notifications and quick settings: drag downward from the very top.
  • Settings: in the list of all apps.

There is no lock screen and the screen does not switch itself off: gameDroid turns both off, since this is a games machine and not a phone in a pocket.

This browser

The browser that comes with this Android is a plain one, made for testing. It is enough to read a page or sign in somewhere.

  • Go to a page: tap the address bar at the top, type an address, and press Enter.
  • Back: Esc, or Android's Back button.
  • Search: it has no search box. Go to a search site first, or type the site's full address.
  • It has no tabs, bookmarks or downloads list.

To come back to this page, press F3.

Games and apps

  • This Android has no Google Play and no Google apps. That is deliberate: gameDroid is free software and ships none of Google's.
  • Apps come as APK files. Free-software apps and games are collected at f-droid.org.
  • This Android is the x86-64 kind. An app built only for ARM phones will refuse to install or will not start.

Internet

Android believes it is on a Wi-Fi network named VirtWifi. That network is your computer: whatever internet your computer has, Android has, through the same firewall and VPN.

  • Nothing outside can connect in to this Android.
  • If pages do not load, check that your computer itself is online.

What does not work yet

  • Sound. There is none yet, in either direction.
  • Camera, phone calls, text messages, location.
  • Game controllers.
  • Speed. Graphics are drawn by the processor unless gameDroid was started with its graphics-card mode, which is still experimental.
